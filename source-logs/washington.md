- Note on a task-brief expectation: the brief suggested doubled fines in emergency zones. The current Guide does NOT state a doubled fine for emergency zones — it states the Move Over / slow-down rule (§4.17). Doubled fines ARE stated by the Guide for: passing a stopped school bus (§4.2), offences in construction/work zones when workers are present (§4.12), and distracted-driving fines after a first violation (§3.4). The bank tests only what the Guide states.
